## 1. What this experience is

A **short interactive cinematic artwork** that serves as the engagement invitation for **PARSA & SABA**.

It is delivered through a QR code, opened on a phone, and experienced once — probably in a single sitting, probably while standing, probably with other people nearby.

It is **not** a website. It is **not** a landing page. It is **not** a slideshow. It is **not** a demo.

---

## 2. North Star

> ### CINEMATIC LUXURY × FUTURISTIC ROMANCE

The experience must feel:

deeply romantic · emotionally restrained · premium · cinematic · modern · intimate · technologically sophisticated · visually memorable · **never generic**

The target reaction:

> *"I have never received an invitation like this before."*

The reaction we must never provoke:

> *"Nice effects."*

---

## 3. The two governing laws

### Law 1 — Technology exists to serve emotion

No effect exists for its own sake. Every shader, particle, transition, and interaction must answer:

**"What does the guest feel because of this, that they would not feel without it?"**

If the honest answer is *"it looks impressive"*, it does not belong.

### Law 2 — Luxury comes from restraint

A simpler implementation that feels more elegant is **always** preferable to a technically impressive but emotionally empty one.

Negative space, silence, slowness, and absence are primary design materials — not gaps to be filled.

---

## 4. The central visual metaphor

Three identities, expressed as **light**, never as literal imagery.

| | Identity | Expressed as |
|---|---|---|
| **PARSA** | deep violet | controlled energy, strength, precision |
| **SABA** | rose / pearl light | fluidity, softness, calm |
| **TOGETHER** | **gold** | what they create together |

### Critical interpretation rules

- This is **not** "fire animation + water animation". Never literal fire. Never literal water.
- Violet and rose appear as **illumination**, not as large UI surfaces, not as background gradients filling the screen.
- **Gold is earned.** It appears only as a consequence of convergence. It is never a decorative theme color, never a border, never a generic wedding gold.
- Gold should read as **reflected precious metal or physical light**, never as flat yellow.

---

## 5. The visual arc

```
OBSIDIAN BLACK
  → VIOLET / ROSE
    → CONVERGENCE
      → GOLD
```

This arc is the spine of the entire experience. Every beat must be locatable somewhere on it.

If a proposed scene cannot be placed on this arc, it does not belong in the experience.

---

## 6. Core emotional thesis

The couple described their relationship this way:

```
«خیلی سخت بود،
خیلی سخت‌تر هم خواهد بود،
اما ما قوی‌تریم و مهربان‌تریم.
ما باهم،
ولی برای هم.»
```

This is the **emotional center** of the experience.

- Its **meaning is non-negotiable.**
- Its final line breaks, typography, and precise wording **may still be refined** — open item **O-01** in `CREATIVE_LOCK.md` §9.
- It must **not** be blindly translated into English wedding-card language.
- It must **not** be softened into something sweeter. The dignity of *"خیلی سخت‌تر هم خواهد بود"* is the reason the piece is moving. Do not remove the difficulty.

### The thesis translated into visual logic

> **Union does not mean the disappearance of identity.**

Two presences approach. They do **not** merge into one. What is precious — the gold — exists **between** them and **because of** them.

This single idea drives the Signature Moment and, through it, the whole piece.

---

## 7. Visual language

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Environment** | Obsidian black. Deep, not flat pure black. |
| **Space** | Large amounts of negative space. Emptiness is the default state. |
| **Gold** | Reflected precious metal / physical light. Post-convergence only. |
| **Violet & rose** | Illumination, not surfaces. |
| **Typography** | Editorial, sophisticated, restrained. |
| **Photography** | Cinematic, emotionally authentic, graded, never framed like a card. |
| **Motion** | Slow. Breathing. Never snappy, never bouncy, never "web animation". |

---

## 8. ABSOLUTELY AVOID — permanent blacklist

None of the following may appear, in any beat, in any form, at any intensity:

- floating hearts
- excessive sparkles
- confetti
- flower petals
- envelope / letter-opening clichés
- generic wedding templates
- excessive gold decoration
- ornamental borders and flourishes
- cheesy romantic animations
- excessive particles
- "wow" effects with no narrative purpose
- excessive gradients
- generic card-based UI
- unnecessary 3D objects
- a literal 3D wedding ring
- literal fire or water imagery
- technology for technology's sake
- anything that resembles a wedding SaaS template

**On "excessive particles" and "excessive gradients":** the word is *excessive*. A small, purposeful particle event — such as the RSVP message dissolving into a single point of light (`CREATIVE_LOCK.md` §6) — is permitted because it carries narrative meaning. A particle field that exists to look impressive is not. The test is §9, not the particle count.

---

## 9. Anti-cliché tests

Before any visual is accepted, it must pass all four:

1. **The template test** — Could this appear on a generic wedding invitation site? → If yes, reject.
2. **The narrative test** — What emotional beat does this serve? → If the answer is only "it looks good", reject.
3. **The restraint test** — Would removing 30% of this make it better? → If yes, remove it.
4. **The silence test** — Could this moment work with less motion, less sound, less light? → If yes, do that instead.

## 10. The role of the agent

The agent is a **creative collaborator, not an autonomous art director.**

You may — and should — propose better ideas. You may not replace this creative identity with your own aesthetic.

### Deviation protocol

Before making any significant creative deviation, you must present:

1. Current direction
2. Your proposed alternative
3. Why your alternative is better
4. Which creative principle it preserves
5. What it changes
6. Potential risks
7. Recommendation

Then **wait for the owner's decision.** Do not proceed on your own judgment.
