# BEAT 2 — میانِ ما / The Between ★ SIGNATURE MOMENT

Two distant points of light — Parsa precise, Saba fluid — find each other across
obsidian black. Their paths are the event. They never merge. An incomplete luminous
loop forms only once a relationship exists. Gold is a consequence of that meeting,
not a colour behind type. The names arrive after the visual has already said it.

## Interaction

First pointer awakens the piece and starts autoplay. Scroll may take over after that.
Until the first pointer, the frame is black.

Dev: `?at=0.5` holds a frame (also awakens). `?tier=full|reduced|static`.
